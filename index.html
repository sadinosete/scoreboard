How the scoring works
  • Every task you tick adds its points. Every task still open at 00:00 takes its points away, so you need more than half the points on offer to finish a day positive.
  • Your rating is the running total of those daily results. It moves at midnight, can’t fall below 0, and sets your rank. A bad run demotes you.
  • Your rival is you at your recent form. Each day he scores your average from the previous seven days, never less than 60% of the points on offer, and he never skips a day.
  • Score more than him to win the day. A perfect day always wins.
